BOP!BLOCK Privacy Policy

YANGZstudios · Updated 25 November 2026

This policy covers BOP!BLOCK on iOS and Android. You can play without creating an account. Features vary by version.

Saved games

The game saves your board, unfinished round, settings and personal bests on your device. It also keeps a random installation ID and a local log of sessions, moves and ad events for playtesting. Removing the app and its data clears these local records.

Global leaderboards

In versions with leaderboards, the game saves your chosen nickname and a private, randomly generated key on your device. When online, it sends the key, nickname, difficulty and best completed score to our leaderboard service on Cloudflare. A score earned offline can be sent when you reconnect.

We store a hash of the key to match new scores to the correct entry. Each difficulty displays the nicknames and scores of its top ten players. Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver the service and limit repeated submissions. The ranking database stores the nickname, difficulty, score and hashed key.

Deleting the game clears the local key. Scores already submitted remain on the leaderboard service. To request removal or report a nickname, email yangzstudios@gmail.com with the nickname, difficulty and score.

Ads and consent

BOP!BLOCK uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded ads and occasional ads between rounds. Google uses data to deliver and measure ads, diagnose problems and prevent fraud. Its Mobile Ads SDK may process your IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, app launches, ad views and taps, and crash and performance information.

Identifiers can include the Android advertising ID or an identifier specific to an app or developer on iOS. On iPhone and iPad, versions that offer tracking ask for your permission through Apple. If you allow tracking and your other consent choices permit it, Google AdMob may use your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalize ads and measure their performance across other companies’ apps and websites.

If you decline or tracking is restricted, AdMob does not receive your IDFA. You can still play, use leaderboards and earn the same reward for completing an available rewarded ad. Eligible ads may still appear. To change Apple’s tracking permission, open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your device.

Google’s User Messaging Platform manages consent or opt-out choices where applicable. To revisit a choice, open Privacy in the game and select Privacy choices when available. Your choice can affect which ads you see. On Android, you can also manage the advertising ID in your device settings.

Google describes its processing and retention in its Privacy Policy and its guide to partner apps.

Children

BOP!BLOCK is intended for players aged 13 and older. If you believe a child has shared personal information through the game, contact us to request removal.

Support and privacy requests

Email YANGZstudios at yangzstudios@gmail.com. If you contact us, we use the email address and details you provide to respond to your request. YANGZstudios does not sell personal information.
