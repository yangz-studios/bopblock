BOPBLOCK! Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Developer: YANGZstudios

This policy explains what information the BOPBLOCK! mobile game ("the game", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. BOPBLOCK! is a free puzzle game supported by advertising. It has no user accounts, no in-app purchases and no in-game store.

Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the game stores the following only on your device:

  • Your current game, score, personal best and settings (such as sound on or off).
  • A random installation identifier, and simple play statistics such as the number of sessions and completed games, plus game events used to tune difficulty and ad timing.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or other personal files. The game does not send this saved information to us or to any server we operate. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads (for example, to continue a game) and occasional ads between games. To serve, limit, measure and prevent fraud in ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • The device advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) and, where available, Android Privacy Sandbox signals.
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system, language and app version.
  • Interactions with ads, such as views and taps.

Google processes this information under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices and consent

  • Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent message before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice later from the game's privacy settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalization, in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads on some devices).
  • If you decline personalization, you may still see non-personalized ads.
  • The game works offline; ads are simply unavailable without a connection.

Children

BOPBLOCK! is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data sharing, retention and security

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the advertising processing by Google described above, we do not share information with third parties. On-device data stays until you uninstall the game or clear its storage. Google retains advertising data according to its own policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
